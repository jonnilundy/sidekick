The first public release.

- Press ⌥⌘Space, ask, read, press Esc. The card slides in from the right edge and gets out of the way.
- Runs your own `claude` with your login, settings, skills and connectors. Answers come first, with a few "worth knowing" bullets.
- One session all day, reset with ⌘N or at 5 AM. Nothing is saved to disk.
- After 3 quiet minutes it opens as just the field; drag the bottom edge down for earlier questions.
- Markdown renders while it streams: lists, tables, code, alerts, footnotes and math.
- Updates itself through Sparkle, quietly: a dot on ✦ when an update is ready.
