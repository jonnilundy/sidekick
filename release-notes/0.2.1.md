Fixes from a stress test with worst-case data.

- An empty answer now says "No answer came back." instead of showing nothing.
- Click a long question's bubble to read all of it; click again to shorten it.
- Wide tables fade at the right edge, so you can see there is more to scroll.
- ⌘↓ shows earlier questions and ⌘↑ hides them. VoiceOver can reach them too.
- While an answer streams, a half-arrived `**`, `~~` or backtick stays hidden until it closes.
- Numbered lists that start at 1000 or more render as lists.
- Long lists stream more smoothly.
- Fixed a crash when a `<details>` section arrived cut off mid-stream.
